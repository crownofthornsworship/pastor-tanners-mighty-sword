# Pastor Tanner's Mighty Sword — Expansion Roadmap

## Product goal

Deliver a polished ten-level action-adventure where movement, sword combat against shadows, conversation, rescue, and Scripture choices all matter.

## Campaign v1.0 status

The ten-level structure, visible-blade hit timing, directional attacks, five shadow behaviors, ten Scripture rescues, mini-bosses at levels 3, 6, and 9, and a three-stage level 10 finale are now implemented. Progress migrates from the preview save and persists locally.

## Phase 1 — Combat and movement polish

- Split Tanner's sprite sheet into reliable idle, walk, run, sword windup, swing, follow-through, hurt, and victory states.
- Synchronize the sword hitbox with the visible blade and prevent damage outside the swing frames.
- Add directional attacks, brief recovery, knockback, invulnerability feedback, and readable enemy tells.
- Refine touch controls for portrait and landscape without covering the play area.

## Phase 2 — Ten-level campaign

- Levels 1–3: teach movement, conversation, identification, and basic shadow combat.
- Levels 4–6: add hazards, branching paths, residents to protect, and multi-shadow encounters.
- Levels 7–9: combine prior mechanics with optional rescues and hidden Scripture pages.
- Level 10: a multi-stage boss encounter requiring correct Scripture understanding and combat mastery.

## Phase 3 — Enemies and challenges

- Add distinct shadow families with silhouette, motion, audio cue, and counterplay differences.
- Add environmental puzzles, chase sequences, timed protection events, and peaceful exploration breaks.
- Keep people safe and never depict the sword harming a person.
- Add mini-bosses that test one learned mechanic before the finale.

## Phase 4 — Presentation

- Upgrade environments, lighting, landmarks, portraits, dialogue panels, impacts, and transitions.
- Add an illustrated level map, animated chapter intros, completion grades, and a satisfying ending.
- Preserve the approved recognizable Tanner likeness and family-friendly tone.

## Release gate

- Complete all ten levels on desktop and small-phone portrait/landscape layouts.
- Verify collision, sword timing, Scripture answer/retry logic, checkpoints, saves, sound, reduced motion, and touch controls.
- Publish through GitHub Pages and update the game hub, studio, social metadata, and preview image.
